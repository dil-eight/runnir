## Broad Coverage Backlog

Research official programs for error tracking/observability, uptime/status, transactional email, databases/auth/storage, payments/tax, domains/DNS, team security, customer support/CRM, scheduling, documentation, incorporation/accounting, design and customer research. Prioritize tools that map to an explicit repository need or user goal. Corporate/legal/financial services require user context and separate suitability review; a codebase cannot establish eligibility.

Compare free/open-source/non-affiliate options alongside paid offers. Verify commercial usage, region, company stage, new-account rules, expiry, credit stacking, overages, cancellation/renewal, data processing terms and permitted promotion channels. Do not claim every useful service has a referral program. Record unverified programs as research-only and keep them out of the live catalog.

## Catalog Operations

Maintain the live register outside the build with a review owner, verification date, source links, expiry, enrollment status, approved URL and disclosure. Re-check before publication and expire stale offers. Keep payout details and affiliate account operations separate from local relevance ranking. Do not create affiliate accounts, scrape user profiles or enroll users automatically.
